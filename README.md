# snackhana-digital-marketing-capstone
A strategic digital marketing capstone for positioning Snackhana as a modern healthy snack brand through consumer personas, competitive analysis, funnel strategy, SEO, paid media, social media, content, email marketing, and marketing automation.
### Snackhana — Digital Marketing Capstone Project

**Category:** Academic Project | Digital Marketing | Growth Strategy

As part of my PGDM coursework at FIIB, I collaborated with a team to develop a strategic digital marketing approach for Snackhana, a modern healthy-snacking brand concept focused on products such as crispy chips and roasted makhana.

**Project Highlights**
- **Brand Positioning:** Identified market gaps and developed a differentiated positioning strategy for everyday healthy snacking.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Created customer personas and mapped their needs, preferences, and snacking challenges.
- **SEO & Keyword Research:** Identified relevant search keywords and explored search intent and estimated advertising costs.
- **Paid Advertising:** Planned Google Search and Meta advertising campaigns with defined objectives, audiences, budgets, and ad creatives.
- **Marketing Funnel:** Developed TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU strategies to support awareness, consideration, and conversions.
- **Content & Social Media:** Developed content concepts and Instagram campaign creatives.
- **Retention Marketing:** Designed email nurturing, welcome emails, browse-abandonment emails, cart-abandonment emails, and customer communication workflows.

**Key Learning**

The project strengthened my understanding of how brand positioning, audience insights, search marketing, paid advertising, content creation, and retention campaigns can work together within an integrated digital marketing strategy.

**Skills Demonstrated:** Digital Marketing Strategy, SEO, Keyword Research, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Customer Segmentation, Content Marketing, Marketing Funnel Design, Email Marketing, and Campaign Planning.
